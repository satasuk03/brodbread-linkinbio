how to store

Keep it in its paper bag, cut side down, at room temperature — good for 2–3 days. Skip the fridge; it stales the crumb faster. For longer keeping, slice and freeze in a sealed bag for up to a month.

how to reheat

Whole loaf — spritz with water, bake at 180°C for 8–10 minutes (12–15 from frozen). Slices — straight into the toaster or a dry pan. The crust comes back: crackly outside, soft inside.
